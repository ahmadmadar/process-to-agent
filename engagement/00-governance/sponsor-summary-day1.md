## Bottom line

1. **Claims are slow because they wait, not because staff are slow.** The
   longest stage comes after the decision, at finance pre-approval and the
   weekly payment run (Q4, Q6). Hands-on work is about three hours a claim
   (T2:L66), an estimate carried as an assumption.
2. **Finance pre-approval almost never changes a claim** (Q5). We recommend
   removing it at or below $10k and for denials, subject to the CFO.
3. **Account-manager payments cost recovery.** Claims decided by account
   managers are paid before documents are complete far more often, and
   recover far less from carriers (Q11). **This needs your decision** (see
   Ratification).
4. **The agent cannot pay everything under $50k.** That line (T1:L12)
   includes claims above the insurance deductible, where the insurer must be
   told and must consent before we settle (T2:L16; Q7). The one claim paid
   before notice lost its cover (Q12).

## Decisions made

| Ruling | Decision |
|---|---|
| C1 | Delay is waiting time; the ~3 hours of hands-on work is an assumption, not a measurement |
| C2, E1, E2 | Finance pre-approval kept only above $10k; removed at or below $10k and for denials (CFO to agree) |
| C3 | Recovery baseline is recovered ÷ paid across all paid claims; the accepted-claims rate is reported alongside |
| C4, E4 | The claims team owns every claim payout; account managers request expedited handling but do not approve or pay (**your ratification needed**) |
| C5, C5a–c | The agent works in three tiers: autonomous approvals at or below $10k with complete documents; human sign-off for all denials and partials, and for claims above $10k up to the deductible; immediate escalation for temperature claims, cold-chain customers, expedite requests and claims above the deductible |
| E3 | Recommendation to finance: pay on approval, or run payments more often than weekly |
| E5 | Document chasing redesigned: a checklist and deadline at intake, automated reminders |
| E6 | Clean-POD shortages not filed with carriers. **Reversed on Day 2 (E6a)** after the data showed carriers do pay them (Q18) |
| S1 | Loss claims share the redesigned process but are outside the agent pilot |

## Conflicts resolved

  work (T1:L24) is not supported by the stage data (Q4).

  on different bases (Q9). We report both.

  the insurer reason above.
- **Disagreements (C6).** There is conflict over claim decisions that does not
  reach you, including the insurer claim that lost cover (T2:L16; Q12).
