## Please confirm

1. **Account managers cannot approve or pay claims; they may submit expedite requests.**
2. **Same-day answer to expedite requests (business days, Eastern Time; public holidays are not business days):** requests received before 14:00 are answered by the end of that business day. Requests received at or after 14:00 are answered by 12:00 the next business day. The claims lead is still to confirm capacity.
3. **What the same-day answer can say:** the claim's current status, the next step, and the expected date where known. The answer can promise payment only once the claims team has approved the claim (for example, "approved, payment going out [date]"). Before that, the account manager cannot tell the customer "we're paying, it's in hand". Payment is not committed before the claim is decided, before the documents are complete, or before the insurer has agreed where its agreement is needed.
