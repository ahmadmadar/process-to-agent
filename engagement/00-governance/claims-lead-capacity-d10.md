# Capacity Check: Expedite Response Commitment (D10)

> Fictional engagement for portfolio purposes. The client, stakeholders and data are synthetic.

**To:** Claims and dispatch lead · **From:** AI lead consultant · **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Bottom line

The COO has agreed that the claims team owns every claim payout, on the
condition that every account-manager expedite request gets a same-day answer
and that you own it, with the senior specialist as backup (T3:L25, T3:L30).
You were not in that session. Before the requirements are frozen, please
confirm whether the team can meet the commitment below, or tell us what it
would take.

## The commitment

- **Hours:** business days, Eastern Time. Public holidays are not business days.
- **Before 14:00:** answered by the end of that business day.
- **At or after 14:00:** answered by 12:00 the next business day.
- **What an answer is:** the claim's current status, the next step, and the expected date where known. It can promise payment only once the claims team has approved the claim.
- **If it is missed:** the system notifies you and the requesting account manager.

## Please tell us

1. Can you and the senior specialist meet this alongside your current split of roughly 60% claims and 40% dispatch (T2:L24)?
2. If not, what would need to change: the cut-off, the backup, or something else?
